Where your data goes

Your Plex server address and access token, password verifiers, indexed titles, performer credits, review history and downloaded portraits are stored locally on the Plex Cast Explorer server, normally under C:\ProgramData\PlexCastExplorer. It does not include an application-operated cloud account or telemetry service. Plex tokens are used to connect to your chosen Plex server, not transmitted to portrait providers.

If you configure ThePornDB with your own API key, optional image searches send performer names and related filmography information to that third-party service, whose own privacy policy applies. Do not use these searches unless you have permission under the provider's terms and the relevant image and publicity rights. TMDB and Wikimedia lookups are unavailable in version 1.0.

Security

The built-in server uses unencrypted HTTP. Use it on a trusted LAN or over a properly secured private VPN. Never expose its HTTP port directly to the public internet. Other LAN participants may observe traffic. The administrator is responsible for access control, protecting local backup files and regularly updating the server.
