## Owner's intended policy

- Publicly viewable and shareable source, available for personal or otherwise genuinely noncommercial use.
- **All commercial use prohibited** without written authorization, **including free internal deployment by for-profit businesses**.
- Distributed copies of the Program must include the same license and corresponding source.
- Modified versions accessed by other people over a network must publicly expose their corresponding source.
- Privately edited copies that are not distributed or made accessible to other people over a network are not subject to mandatory publication. This is a practical scope limit, not a promise to capture every private improvement.
- No license to provider metadata, third-party portraits, or trademarks.

## Ongoing rights and licensing considerations

1. Confirm the real individual/legal entity that owns the original program copyright; update copyright lines and a contact for commercial authorization. A GitHub handle may not suffice for agreements.
2. Review the breadth of "Commercial Use" and "material benefit," especially educational nonprofits, consultants, businesses using the Program internally for no fee, and people with mixed personal/commercial activity.
3. Validate enforceability/clarity of the network disclosure trigger, public-location requirement, remedies, and termination/cure provisions in relevant jurisdictions.
4. Consider whether to provide an express patent license, jurisdiction/venue, a formal written-permission process, and a commercial exception template.
5. Have outside contributors execute contributor terms granting the maintainer the rights needed to relicense their contributions commercially; if not, those contributions may limit future permissions. Do not accept PRs on a broad commercial-relicense assumption.
6. Audit all third-party code and assets for compatible licensing and redistribution permissions. Application licensing does not alter TMDB/provider API terms.
7. Keep the adopted root `LICENSE`, public README, pull-request guidance, and releases consistent.

## Selected reference points (for context, not license incorporation)

- [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0): established noncommercial wording, but it does not on its own impose the requested network source-sharing duty.
- [GNU AGPLv3 §13](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html): network-source obligation for modified versions, but AGPLv3 permits commercial use and is **not** the license used for this project.
- [OSI Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd): prohibits discriminating against commercial fields of endeavor, so this project is **source-available**, not OSI open-source.
- [GitHub legal guide](https://opensource.guide/legal/): contributor ownership, licensing and optional CLAs.
